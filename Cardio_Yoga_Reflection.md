Cardio + Yoga Reflection

For the cardio part I went with an incline walk on the treadmill for 35 minutes. I picked walking because my cardio is honestly pretty bad and I knew if I tried to jog for 30 minutes straight I'd end up breathless, which is exactly what the assignment said not to do. I've been lifting for a few years so I'm decently strong, but anything endurance based wears me out fast. I set it to a 4% incline at about 3.3 mph and mostly stayed there. That put me right around a 13 on the exertion scale. My breathing was up and I was sweating, but I could still talk in full sentences if I had to. Around the 20 minute mark it started creeping up so I dropped the incline a little for a few minutes to keep it steady instead of pushing through. That's new for me. I'm used to just grinding, so paying attention to the talk test and backing off on purpose felt weird but it made sense given the injuries chapter we're on.

Then I did the 22 minute yoga video. I was not excited about this part. I've never really done yoga and I'm stiff from years of lifting without stretching much. Turns out it was fine, and I actually kind of liked it. The pace was slow and easy to follow, and it was a good cooldown after the walk. My hips and hamstrings were the tightest, and I had to modify a couple of the poses because I couldn't get as deep as the instructor. The breathing part was probably the most useful thing for me since I tend to hold my breath when I'm working hard.

Overall the whole thing took right around an hour. I liked the cardio more than I expected because it was steady and I never felt wrecked, which is not my usual experience with cardio. It also reminded me how far behind my conditioning is compared to my strength. I want to keep doing these steady state walks a few times a week and slowly bump the incline up. The yoga I'll probably keep too, at least once a week, since my mobility clearly needs it.
