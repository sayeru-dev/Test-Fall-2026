Workout Reflection

Honestly this workout was harder than I expected, but not for the reasons I thought it would be. I've been lifting for about three years, mostly powerlifting and bodybuilding style stuff, so the actual exercises were nothing new. Squats, lunges, push-ups, shoulder press, curls, I can do all of that no problem. The issue is my cardio is pretty bad. I'm 194 lbs and I get winded on a light incline walk, so doing eight exercises back to back for three rounds was a whole different thing.

I watched the videos first and did a quick 7 minute walk to warm up. Even that got my heart rate going a bit. The first round felt easy and I probably went too fast because of it. Then I got to the mountain climbers and that's when it fell apart. About halfway through the 30 seconds I was gassed. Bicycle crunches right after that were rough too. By the second and third rounds I was dropping reps on the squats and push-ups and taking longer breaks between rounds just to catch my breath. I ended up doing the lunges without weights on the last round so I could focus on breathing and form. Nothing ever felt heavy, I just couldn't keep up the pace.

The core stuff surprised me as well. I only recently started doing real ab and lower back work, and the crunches and bicycles burned way more than they should for someone who can squat decent weight. I've clearly been skipping that side of things.

I did the full 20 minute stretch at the end, which I never do after lifting. Glad I did. My hips and hamstrings were tight from the lunges and it helped me cool down instead of just stopping cold.

Main takeaway is that my strength is way ahead of my conditioning. I already knew I was below average on endurance for my age but this made it really obvious. Going forward I want to add a couple short cardio sessions each week, keep doing circuits like this one, and try to shrink my rest between rounds over time. I also want to keep the core and functional work in my routine instead of treating it like an afterthought. This wasn't hard in the way I'm used to, it was hard in the way I actually need to work on.
